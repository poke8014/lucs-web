# Gardening for Beginners — Source Summary

**Author:** BBC Gardeners' World Magazine
**Published:** undated
**URL:** https://www.gardenersworld.com/how-to/grow-plants/gardening-for-beginners-10-tips/
**Clipped:** 2026-05-15

## What this source contributes

A tightly-condensed 10-tip primer covering the *care* end of gardening — watering, feeding, planting depth, pruning, composting, pest tolerance, wildlife. Its main contribution to the wiki is on the **care** axis where the existing PPD sources are thinner: the rootball-weekly watering technique, fortnightly feeding cadence in growing season, planting depth (rootball flush with grade), and the "start small + suppress the rest with cardboard or black plastic" approach.

UK context — feeding/pruning cadence and pest pressure transfer reasonably to mild-summer CA; cardboard-suppression is universal.

## Topics covered

1. **Know your garden** — aspect, soil type, soil test, looking at neighboring plants for soil pH clues.
2. **Plan your garden** — sit down before buying plants.
3. **Learn how to plant** — proper depth (rootballs flush, not buried or proud), weed and prep soil first.
4. **Feed and water plants regularly** — water the **rootball**, not leaves; **soak weekly** rather than sprinkle daily; feed fortnightly in spring/summer (more often in containers).
5. **Start small** — cover the area you're not yet ready to cultivate with **cardboard or black plastic** to suppress weeds while you work elsewhere.
6. **Keep an eye on pests** — most don't need action; predators handle them; intervene only on real infestations.
7. **Make use of compost** — break down for ~a year, then mulch base of plants.
8. **Don't be afraid to prune** — learn timing and cuts; rewards are better growth and flowering/fruiting.
9. **Be kind to wildlife** — birds eat slugs/aphids, bees pollinate, frogs/toads/hedgehogs are part of the system.
10. **Enjoy your garden** — sitting area, bird feeders.

## Contradictions and corroborations

- **Soak weekly, don't sprinkle daily** — strongly aligned with the [[concepts/watering]] page's existing "deep infrequent watering" guidance for drought-tolerant plants. Source generalizes this for all plants, not just drought-adapted; the wiki page now reflects both framings.
- **Feed fortnightly in growing season** — new claim; UK / generic context. Likely **does not apply to established CA natives**, which evolved on lean soils and can be harmed by feeding. Flagged on the watering page with a forward-link to a future native-care page.
- **"Start small + cardboard suppression"** — new. Pairs nicely with the parallel-tracks idea from [planning/sunshower/CONTEXT.md](../../planning/sunshower/CONTEXT.md): a user can cardboard-suppress one bed while site-inventorying or removing weeds in another.
