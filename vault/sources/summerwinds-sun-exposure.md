# How to Determine Sun Exposure In Your Yard — Source Summary

**Author:** SummerWinds Nursery (Arizona)
**Published:** undated
**URL:** https://www.summerwindsnursery.com/az/inspire/blog/guide-to-mapping-garden-sun-exposure/
**Clipped:** 2026-05-15

## What this source contributes

A narrowly-scoped how-to on sun-mapping a yard. Two contributions to the wiki beyond what existing sources already cover:

1. **A 5-tier sun map vocabulary** (full sun / morning-sun-afternoon-shade / morning-shade-afternoon-sun / dappled shade / full shade), refining the 3-tier `full | part | shade` schema used in plant frontmatter.
2. **The hourly-photo sun-mapping technique** — set a phone alarm every hour from ~8am to 8pm on a sunny day and take a yard photo each time; compare afterward.

Also reinforces hydrozoning — *"group plants together that have similar watering needs"* — which is a recurring concept worth its own page if a second source corroborates.

Arizona desert context — plant suggestions (vinca, hibiscus, sweet potato as ornamental tuber) don't transfer to CA native gardening, but the sun-mapping methodology is portable.

## Topics covered

1. **Cardinal directions** — south-facing = most sun; north-facing = least; east = morning sun; west = afternoon sun.
2. **Compound aspects** — SE, NW, etc. blend the two cardinal patterns.
3. **Tree-induced shade** — dappled shade as a distinct category from full sun or full shade.
4. **Sun mapping** — hourly photo technique; tabulating 5 labeled zones across the yard.
5. **Hydrozoning** — group plants with similar water needs, regardless of sun zone.

## Contradictions and corroborations

- **3-tier vs 5-tier sun categories** — refinement, not contradiction. The 5-tier vocabulary is *more specific* in the part-sun middle (which morning/afternoon, and whether dappled). For frontmatter purposes the 3-tier scheme is sufficient; the 5-tier is useful when interpreting nursery plant tags or planning placement in inland California heat (where afternoon-sun-tolerance is much harder than morning-sun-tolerance).
- **Hydrozoning** — first source to name it explicitly. Doesn't get its own page until a second source corroborates.
