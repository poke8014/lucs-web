# Flower Gardening For Beginners — Source Summary

**Author:** Amy Fedele (Pretty Purple Door)
**Published:** 2020-05-01
**URL:** https://www.prettypurpledoor.com/flower-gardening-for-beginners/
**Clipped:** 2026-05-07

## What this source contributes

A foundational beginner's guide to *plant-side* gardening knowledge — the inputs a gardener needs before they can intelligently pick or care for plants. Pairs with the same author's [[sources/design-a-garden-layout]], which covers *site-side* layout. Together the two articles supply most of the horticultural-layer content of [[concepts/right-plant-right-place]].

Generic — not native-CA-specific, not pollinator-specific. The author's frame is "things I wish someone had told me when I started." Useful as scaffolding for foundational concepts; later sources will need to override it for CA-native specifics (especially watering and soil amendment, where the generic advice diverges sharply from native-CA practice).

## Topics covered

1. **USDA hardiness zones** — the zone system, how to read a zone tag, how to find your zone.
2. **Perennial / annual / biennial** — life-cycle classification (biennials only named, not developed).
3. **Sun levels** — full sun (≥6h), part sun (3-6h), shade (≤3h).
4. **Soil** — texture (sandy / clay), the squeeze test, pH testing, and the "amend less, choose better" principle.
5. **Plant spacing** — radius math, foundation planting offsets, planting for mature size.
6. **Plant care** — watering (1"/week, ~0.6 gal/sq ft, establishment period), fertilizing (mostly product recommendations — skipped from concept treatment), and frost/cold protection (last-frost dates, "delicate" zone-edge plants, frost cloth).
7. **How to plant** — previewed only; refers out to a separate article.

## Contradictions and corroborations

- **Sun thresholds** — corroborated. [[sources/design-a-garden-layout]] said "full sun ≥6h, full shade <3h"; this source says the same and adds the part-sun middle category (3-6h). No contradiction; refined.
- **"Right plant, right place"** — corroborated. Explicitly named as the motto in this source; mentioned in passing in the prior source.
- **Soil amendment** — flagged as **possibly inapplicable to CA natives**. The source recommends amending with compost where convenient and choosing plants that match existing soil where not. For California natives in particular, compost-rich beds may *harm* drought-adapted natives. Noted on the soil and watering pages; revisit when a CA-native source addresses this directly.
- **1"/week watering rule** — flagged as **likely incorrect for established CA natives**. Many CA natives die from summer water once established. Noted on the watering page.
