# A Beginner's Guide to Designing a Garden — Source Summary

**Author:** Melissa (Little Terraced House blog)
**Published:** 2021-04-22
**URL:** https://littleterracedhouse.com/2021/04/22/a-beginners-guide-to-designing-a-garden/
**Clipped:** 2026-05-15

## What this source contributes

A homeowner-narrated guide to designing a small (~6m × 16m) UK garden from scratch. Strongest contribution to the wiki is the **garden-zoning** framing — organizing the yard into named functional areas (potting, seating, lawn/borders, sunny patio, veg patch) before any planting decisions. Complements the [[sources/design-a-garden-layout]] technical layout framework with a lived-in "this is how the rooms actually came out" perspective.

UK context — some plant-specific advice (acers, RHS plant tags, ericaceous compost for blueberries) doesn't transfer directly to native-CA gardening, but the design framework is portable.

## Topics covered

1. **Site survey** — sketch, aspect (compass app), sun tracking through the day, wind exposure.
2. **Garden zoning** — break the yard into functional "rooms" matched to microclimate (potting area in cold-but-morning-sun spot, family dining in sheltered area, veg patch in full sun).
3. **Work in logical order** — clear unwanted features → lights → fences → landscaping → furniture → borders → **plants last**. The author's regret: planting before infrastructure means working around climbers forever.
4. **Wildlife framing** — strong advocate for tolerating wildlife (insects, snails, spiders included), avoiding any `-cides` products, and rejecting plastic grass / plastic "living walls."
5. **Trees in every garden** — even small yards can fit a tree, including dwarf fruit varieties or potted olives.
6. **Soil and water** — clay-improvement via annual bark mulching; water butts on guttering; UK rainfall context.
7. **Plant categories** — climbers, borders (layered by height — tall back, short front, mix of perennials + annuals + evergreens), pots.

## Contradictions and corroborations

- **Site survey first** — corroborated. Same conclusion as [[sources/design-a-garden-layout]] and [[sources/flower-gardening-for-beginners]].
- **"Plants last" sequencing** — new. The earlier PPD sources don't explicitly call this out; this source's regret-narrative makes the case viscerally.
- **No plastic grass / no -cides** — first source to take a strong wildlife stance. Aligns with Sunshower's [[concepts/right-plant-right-place]] ecological reading and the project's friendly-to-natives tone direction (see [planning/sunshower/CONTEXT.md](../../planning/sunshower/CONTEXT.md)).
- **"Every garden should have a tree"** — single-source claim; reasonable but no need to elevate beyond mention.
