# Scirpus microcarpus (Panicled Bulrush)

Scirpus microcarpus (Panicled Bulrush) is a California-native grass of Chaparral, Forest, Grassland, Wetland/Riparian, Woodland. - **Flowering season:** Spring

## Grow

- **Sun:** Partial Shade
- **Water:** Moderate, High
- **Soil drainage:** Standing
- **Soil notes:** Adaptable, tolerant of sand, loam and clay
- **Tips:** Plant in sandy, loamy or clay soils that are at least 12 inches deep, with a pH of 5.4-7.4. It is tolerant of anaerobic soils, intolerant of calcareous soils, and tolerant of infertile soils. Scirpus microcarpus is not drought tolerant. It requires a minimum of 12 inches of water per year, and a maximum of 38 inches of water per year once established. Requires moist soil. It does not tolerate full shade well. Prefers part shade. Likes wet soils.
