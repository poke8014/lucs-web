# Corethrogyne filaginifolia (California Aster)

Corethrogyne filaginifolia (California Aster) is a California-native perennial of Chaparral, Coastal Scrub, Woodland. Calscape records **5** butterfly/moth species supported. _Structured data only — full description and named host species pending the Calscape page-scrape enrichment pass (see [[sources/calscape]])._

## Identify

- **Form:** Mounding, Spreading
- **Height:** 8 - 40 in
- **Width:** 1 - 6 ft
- **Flower color:** Pink, Purple, White
- **Flowering season:** Summer, Fall, Winter
- **Seasonality:** Evergreen
- **Fragrance:** None

## Grow

- **Sun:** Full Sun, Partial Shade
- **Water:** Very Low
- **Summer irrigation:** Max 1x / week once established, Never irrigate once established
- **Soil drainage:** Fast, Medium, Slow
- **Soil pH:** 6.0 - 8.0
- **Soil notes:** Tolerant of sand and clay
- **Ease of care:** Moderate
