- **Tips:** Plant in soil that is at least 42 inches deep, with a pH of 4.5-7.5. It prefers deep rich loamy soils in valley floors, but tolerates sandy and clay soils as well. It does well if planted near irrigated areas, streams or seeps, or in areas where the water table is not far below the surface, as long as it's not in permanently waterlogged soils.
