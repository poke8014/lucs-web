# Melica californica (California Melicgrass)

Melica californica (California Melicgrass) is a California-native grass of Forest, Woodland. Calscape records **1** butterfly/moth species supported. _Structured data only — full description and named host species pending the Calscape page-scrape enrichment pass (see [[sources/calscape]])._

## Identify

- **Form:** Upright, Fountain
- **Height:** 1 - 4 ft
- **Width:** 1 ft
- **Flower color:** Yellow, Green, Purple
- **Flowering season:** Summer
- **Fragrance:** None

## Grow

- **Sun:** Full Sun, Partial Shade
- **Water:** Low, Very Low
- **Summer irrigation:** Max 1x / week once established
- **Soil drainage:** Medium
- **Soil pH:** 5.5 - 7.5
- **Soil notes:** Tolerates a variety of soils as long as drainage is good
- **Ease of care:** Easy
- **Tips:** Plant in sandy or loamy soils that are at least 10 inches deep, with a pH of 6-7.5. It is intolerant of anaerobic soils, intolerant of calcareous soils, and tolerant of infertile soils. Melica californica is moderately drought tolerant. It requires a minimum of 14 inches of water per year, and a maximum of 24 inches of water per year once established. Requires fast draining soil. It is moderately tolerant of full shade. Prefers sun or part shade. Likes slopes.
