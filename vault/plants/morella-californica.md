# Morella californica (California Wax Myrtle)

Morella californica (California Wax Myrtle) is a California-native shrub of Coastal Scrub, Forest, Wetland/Riparian. ## Grow

- **Sun:** Full Sun, Partial Shade
- **Water:** Low, Moderate
- **Summer irrigation:** Max 1x / week once established
- **Soil drainage:** Medium
- **Soil pH:** 4.0 - 8.0
- **Soil notes:** Tolerates a variety of soils
- **Ease of care:** Easy
- **Propagation:** For propagating by seed: 2 to 3 months stratification
- **Tips:** Plant in sandy or loamy soils that are at least 20 inches deep, with a pH of 6-7.5. It is intolerant of anaerobic soils, moderately tolerant of calcareous soils, and tolerant of infertile soils. Morella californica is only slightly drought tolerant. It requires a minimum of 40 inches of water per year, and a maximum of 70 inches of water per year once established. Requires moist soil. Prefers shade. Likes sandy soils.
