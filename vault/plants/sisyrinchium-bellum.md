# Sisyrinchium bellum (Blue-eyed Grass)

Sisyrinchium bellum (Blue-eyed Grass) is a California-native perennial of Chaparral, Coastal Scrub, Desert, Forest, Grassland, Meadow, Wetland/Riparian, Woodland. - **Soil drainage:** Fast, Medium, Slow
- **Soil pH:** 5 . 0 - 8 . 0

- **Ease of care:** Easy

- **Tips:** Blue eyed grass grows well in a wide variety of soils - clay, loam or sand. While it prefers moist soils, and meadows or grasslands with plenty of ground water. Once established, it can survive on as little as 8" of rain per year. It handles regular irrigation and seasonal flooding without a problem as well. It likes sun or part shade.
