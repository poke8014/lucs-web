- **Flower color:** Brown, Cream, Green, Lavender, White
- **Flowering season:** Spring, Summer

## Grow

- **Water:** Low
- **Soil drainage:** Fast, Medium, Slow
- **Soil notes:** Prefers sandy or loamy soils.  Does not grow well in clay soils.
- **Ease of care:** Moderate
- **Tips:** Plant in sandy or loamy soils that are at least 10 inches deep, with a pH of 6-8. It is intolerant of anaerobic soils, moderately tolerant of calcareous soils, and requires only moderately fertile soils. Poa secunda is extremely drought tolerant. It requires a minimum of 8 inches of water per year, and a maximum of 16 inches of water per year once established. It is moderately tolerant of shade.
