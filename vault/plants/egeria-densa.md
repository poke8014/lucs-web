# Egeria densa (Brazilian _Egeria_)

Egeria densa (Brazilian Egeria) is a common aquatic perennial (family Hydrocharitaceae) that occurs in lakes, springs, ponds, and streams. Its underwater growth significantly retards water flow and decreases the abundance and diversity of native plant seeds in lake bottoms. It was introduced as a freshwater aquarium plant.

> ⚠ Stub page — generated from Cal-IPC profile + PAF only. Identify / Remove / Prevent sections will be populated once UC Davis WRIC management notes and photos are ingested.

## Why it's a problem

**Q1.1:** Slows water flow. Causes increased sedimentation and nutrient loading.

**Q1.2:** Reduces the abundance and diversity of native plant seeds in lake bottoms.
