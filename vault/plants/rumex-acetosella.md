# Rumex acetosella (sheep sorrel)

Rumex acetosella (red sorrel, sheep sorrel) is a perennial forb/herb (family Polygonaceae). In California, sheep sorrel occurs in a freshwater marsh community and is common in annual grasslands, montane meadows and perennial bunchgrass communities.

> ⚠ Stub page — generated from Cal-IPC profile + PAF only. Identify / Remove / Prevent sections will be populated once UC Davis WRIC management notes and photos are ingested.

## Why it's a problem

**Q1.2:** Displaces native grasses and forbs by root competition, shading. Can form large dense stands. May reduce or eliminate mycorrhizae.
