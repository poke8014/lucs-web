# Ceanothus papillosus (Wartleaf Ceanothus)

Ceanothus papillosus (Wartleaf Ceanothus) is a California-native shrub of Chaparral, Forest. Calscape records **87** butterfly/moth species supported. _Structured data only — full description and named host species pending the Calscape page-scrape enrichment pass (see [[sources/calscape]])._

- **Height:** 6 - 16 ft
- **Flower color:** Blue, Purple
- **Flowering season:** Spring, Winter
- **Seasonality:** Evergreen

## Grow

- **Sun:** Full Sun
- **Water:** Very Low, Low
- **Soil notes:** Prefers sandy or loamy soils.  Does not grow well in clay soils.
