# Euthamia occidentalis (Western Goldenrod)

## Grow

- **Sun:** Full Sun, Partial Shade
- **Water:** Low, Very Low
- **Summer irrigation:** Keep moist
- **Soil drainage:** Fast, Medium, Slow, Standing
- **Soil notes:** Tolerant of a variety of garden soils as long as sufficient moisture is available
- **Tips:** Plant in sandy, loamy or clay soils that are at least 10 inches deep, with a pH of 4.5-7.
