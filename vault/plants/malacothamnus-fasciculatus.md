# Malacothamnus fasciculatus (Chaparral Mallow)

Malacothamnus fasciculatus (Chaparral Mallow) is a California-native shrub of Chaparral, Coastal Scrub, Woodland. If you do water during the summer, make sure you do it in the very early morning, and during a stretch of cooler days in the summer season.
