# Cynoglossum officinale (common houndstongue)

Cynoglossum officinale (houndstongue) is a biennial forb (family Boraginaceae) whose foliage can be toxic to horses and other livestock when it contaminates hay. In California, it is found in the Cascade Ranges. It is native to Eurasia. The seeds have hooked projections that facilitate long distance dispersal by animals and humans.

## Why it's a problem

**Q1.1:** no information

**Q1.2:** Forms dense stands. Allelopathic. Extracts of hound's-tongue inhibited seed germination and root elongation of wheatgrass (1).
