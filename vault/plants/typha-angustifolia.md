# Typha angustifolia (Narrowleaf Cattail)

Typha angustifolia (Narrowleaf Cattail) is a California-native perennial of Wetland/Riparian. Calscape records **6** butterfly/moth species supported. _Structured data only — full description and named host species pending the Calscape page-scrape enrichment pass (see [[sources/calscape]])._

## Identify

- **Form:** Upright
- **Height:** 5 ft
- **Flower color:** Green
- **Flowering season:** Spring
- **Seasonality:** Winter Deciduous

## Grow

- **Sun:** Full Sun
- **Soil notes:** Adaptable, tolerant of sand, loam and clay
- **Tips:** Plant in sandy, loamy or clay soils that are at least 10 inches deep, with a pH of 3.7-8.5. It is tolerant of anaerobic soils, intolerant of calcareous soils, and requires only moderately fertile soils. Typha angustifolia is only slightly drought tolerant. It requires a minimum of 30 inches of water per year, and a maximum of 60 inches of water per year once established. Requires moist soil. It does not tolerate shade well. Prefers sun. Likes dry granitic slopes .
