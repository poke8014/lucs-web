# Castilleja affinis (Indian Paintbrush)

Castilleja affinis (Indian Paintbrush) is a California-native perennial of Coastal Scrub. Calscape records **15** butterfly/moth species supported. _Structured data only — full description and named host species pending the Calscape page-scrape enrichment pass (see [[sources/calscape]])._

## Identify

- **Form:** Upright
- **Height:** 2 ft
- **Flower color:** Orange, Red, Yellow

## Grow

- **Sun:** Full Sun
- **Soil pH:** 5.5 - 8.2
- **Soil notes:** Rocky soil with sand.
- **Ease of care:** Challenging
- **Propagation:** Since Castilleja is a hemi-parasite, planting it along with host plants such as lupine or bunch grasses may improve growth. Host plants are often very specific and can be difficult to choose, but try associates found in Castilleja's natural community such as Eriophyllum stachaedifolium (Lizard tail), Eriogonum parvifolium (Seacliff buckwheat), or Erigeron glaucus (Seaside daisy).
- **Tips:** Prefers sun. Likes sandy soils.

## Wildlife value

- **Pollinators:** butterflies
- **Butterflies & moths supported:** 15 (named species pending enrichment)
- **Attracts wildlife:** Butterflies, Caterpillars

## Where it belongs

- **Plant communities:** Coastal Strand
- **Cold hardiness:** Tolerates cold to 5° F
- **Site type:** Sea bluffs, hillsides,dry places
- **Nursery availability:** Rarely Available
