# Juncus bufonius (Toad Rush)

Juncus bufonius (Toad Rush) is a California-native grass of Wetland/Riparian. Calscape records **7** butterfly/moth species supported. _Structured data only — full description and named host species pending the Calscape page-scrape enrichment pass (see [[sources/calscape]])._

## Identify

- **Form:** Upright
- **Height:** 1 ft
- **Flower color:** Green
- **Flowering season:** Spring

## Grow

- **Sun:** Full Sun
- **Water:** Moderate, High
- **Summer irrigation:** Keep moist
- **Soil drainage:** Fast, Medium, Slow
- **Soil pH:** 5.6 - 8.0
- **Soil notes:** Tolerant of a variety of garden soils as long as sufficient moisture is available
- **Ease of care:** Moderate
- **Tips:** Plant in sandy, loamy or clay soils that are at least 6 inches deep, with a pH of 4.6-7.6. It is tolerant of anaerobic soils, moderately tolerant of calcareous soils, and tolerant of infertile soils. Juncus bufonius is only slightly drought tolerant. It requires a minimum of 10 inches of water per year, and a maximum of 55 inches of water per year once established. Requires moist soil. It does not tolerate shade well. Prefers sun.
