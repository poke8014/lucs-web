# WRIC Weed Reports — Archive Manifest

**Source:** UC Davis Weed Research and Information Center (WRIC) Weed Reports — natural-areas weed management notes.
**Hosted at:** [Box folder](https://ucdavis.box.com/s/t266vkfh1ym7bb7j57k5ufkrv9vrby3v) (UC Davis public share, owner Kevin S Taniguchi).
**Landing page:** <https://wric.ucdavis.edu/weed-management-notes>
**Captured:** 2026-05-08
**Archive size:** 274 PDFs across 23 alphabetic subfolders (A–Z; no K/Q/Y).

## Background

WRIC migrated reports to Box while the website is under construction (note dated Nov 21, 2025 on the WRIC site). The legacy `wric.ucdavis.edu/information/natural areas/...` URLs that Cal-IPC species pages still link to all 302 to the WRIC landing page now. Reports are PDFs, typically one per genus, with multi-species files when several congeners share management notes (e.g. `Cytisus.pdf` covers C. scoparius and C. striatus; `Hedera_canariensis-helix-hibernica.pdf` covers all three ivies).

PDFs are AES-encrypted (open-without-password) — viewable but not directly text-extractable; ingestion will need a PDF-aware reader.
