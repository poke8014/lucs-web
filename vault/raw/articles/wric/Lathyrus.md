Lathyrus latifolius

Everlasting peavine

Family: Fabaceae

| Grazing | P | can be toxic to livestock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P | as a perennial it will regrow after burning |
| Mowing and cutting | P | mow before FLW, matures early in the season, mowing difficult do to long,viney stems |
| Tillage | G | repeated cultivation can control plants |
| Grubbing,digging or hand pulling | G | pull or dig young plants early before FLW |
