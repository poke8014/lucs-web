Asparagus asparagoides

Bridal creeper

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
Grazing

| Grazing | F | in Australia some control with sheep grazing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P | readily resprouts, may be partially killed with very intense fires |
| Mowing and cutting | P | resprouts readily |
| Tillage | P | will disperse rhizomes and spread plant |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | F | must remove all rhizome fragments |
