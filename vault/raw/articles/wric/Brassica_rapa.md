Brassica rapa

Birdsrape mustard

Family: Brassicaceae

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
GrazingG

| Grazing | G | livestock health problems from grazing brassicas are relatively rare and can largely be avoided by good agronomic and grazing management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P | fire creates conditions favorable to invasion |
| Mowing and cutting | E | cutting at soil surface should be effective |
| Tillage | E | target seedlings and rosettes, germination after tillage is common when soil moisture is present |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | E | remove before seed production |
