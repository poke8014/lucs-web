Agrostis avenacea

Pacific bentgrass

Family: Poaceae

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL

| Grazing | F | sheep may be effective in western US |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P | will kill existing stand but will spread seed in newly disturbed sites |
| Mowing and cutting | P |  |
| Tillage | G |  |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | G |  |
