Elodea canadensis

Common elodea

Family: Hydrocharitaceae (water weed)

| Biological: grass carp | E |
| --- | --- |
| Cultural: benthic barrier | G if barriers are in place by early spring |
| Cultural: drawdown | F unless drawdown allows complete drying of sediments for several weeks. Seed may survive. |
| Cultural: shading | P Plants are well adapted to low light.(Some dyes can be useful if applied early and often) |
| Mechanical: cutting | F but will stimulate regrowth and fragments will spread the plant |
| Mechanical: hand pulling or vacuuming | G |
