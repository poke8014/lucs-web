Shortpod mustard is an erect yellow-flowered mustard to 3 to 4 ft tall. It is a biennial or short-lived
perennial, occasionally a winter annual. The lower leaves of mature plants are obovate, irregularly pinnatelobed and toothed, with the terminal lobe larger than lateral lobes on a long stalk. The upper leaves do not
clasp the stem. The stem bases are moderately to densely covered with stiff, downward-directed hairs. The
basal leaves usually form a rosette, and the leaves are moderately to densely covered with stiff grayish hairs.

| Cultural | Neither grazing nor prescribed burning have been tested for the management of shortpod mustard，but it is not expected that these would be effective。 |
| Biological | Because of its close relationship to important cultivated members of the Brassicaceae，there is no biological control program developed for the management of shortpod mustard。 |
