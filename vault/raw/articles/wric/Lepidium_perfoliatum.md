Lepidium perfoliatum

Clasping pepperweed

Family: Brassicaceae

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
Grazing

| Grazing | F |
| --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P fire creates conditions favorable to invasion |
| Mowing and cutting | P mow shortly before FLW and cut near ground level |
| Tillage | E effective control, germination after tillage is common when soil moisture is available |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | E remove before seed production |
