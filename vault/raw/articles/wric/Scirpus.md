(= Schoenoplectus acutus)

(= Schoenoplectus acutus)

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL

| --- | --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P |  |
| Mowing and cutting | G\* | FLW with all stubble submerged for>2 wks |
| Tillage | P |  |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | G | when soil is moist |
