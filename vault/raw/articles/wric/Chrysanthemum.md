Chrysanthemum coronarium

Crowndaisy

Family: Asteraceae

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
Grazing

| Tillage | E | tillage should control annual if conducted when soil is dry |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | G | annual species should be easily controlled by hand pulling |
