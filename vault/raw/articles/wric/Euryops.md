Euryops multifidus

Sweet resinbush

Family: Asteraceae (sunflower)

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
Cultural: grazing

| Cultural: grazing | P | not considered palatable to livestock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cultural: prescribed burning | G | repeated burning will reduce populations |
| Mechanical: hand removal, weed wrench, cutting | E | does not resprout well |
| Mechanical: heavy equipment removal | F | chaining operations can reduce density |
