Melilotus albus

White sweetclover

Family: Fabaceae

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
Grazing

| Grazing | P | palatable and nutritious to livestock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P | fire creates conditions favorable to invasion |
| Mowing and cutting | P | regrows rapidly if mowed early in the growing season |
| Tillage | E | germination after tillage common when soil moisture is available |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | E | pull or dig young plants in moist soils; difficult to pull when mature |
