Chrysothamnus nauseosus

Rabbitbrush

Family: Asteraceae

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
Grazing

| Grazing | F | often regrows after grazing and may be considered desirable forage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P | considered a fire adapted species that can be enhanced by fire |
| Mowing and cutting | P | can regrow following mowing |
| Tillage | F | must be fairly deep and may need to be repeated |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | E | remove as much of the plant and root as possible to prevent regrowth |
