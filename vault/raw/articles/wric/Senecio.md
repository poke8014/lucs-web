Senecio jacobaea

Tansy ragwort

Family: Asteraceae

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
Grazing

| Grazing | P-F only sheep show tolerance to toxins in plant |
| --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P-G sufficient fuel will provide kill of seedlings, but established plants will resprout |
| Mowing and cutting | P-F side shoots may emerge, must be conducted before FLW |
| Tillage | P root fragments resprout |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | G roots need to be removed, needs to be repeated 2-3 times a year before FLW |
