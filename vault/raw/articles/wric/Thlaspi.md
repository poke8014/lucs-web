Thlaspi arvense

Field pennycress

Family: Brassicaceae

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL
Grazing

| Grazing | P |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P | fire creates conditions favorable to invasion |
| Mowing and cutting | P | mow before FLW, matures early in the season |
| Tillage | E | till before FLW |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | E | remove before FLW |
