Tall fescue

NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL

| Grazing | P |
| --- | --- |
| Prescribed burning | P |
| Mowing and cutting | P |

G = Good control, 80-95%
F = Fair control, 50- 80%

Control is followed by best timing, if known, when efficacy is Eor G.
