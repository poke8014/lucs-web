Agrostis stolonifera

# Creeping bentgrass

Family: Poaceae

# NON-CHEMICAL CONTROL

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| Grazing | P |
| Prescribed burning | PP |
| Mowing and cutting |
| Tillage | P may spread plant as it reproduces from stolons |
| Grubbing, digging or hand pulling | 5 may spread plant as it reproduces from stolons |
