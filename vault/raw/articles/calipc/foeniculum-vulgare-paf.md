| Question 2.4 Innate reproductive potential [?](https://www.cal-ipc.org/plants/paf/foeniculum-vulgare-plant-assessment-form/#help-241) | AOther Published Material |
| Describe key reproductive characteristics:<br>Can reproduce from both crown and seeds. Seeds germinate at almost any time of year, but plants generally do not flower until they are 18 mo. to two years old. Seeds are produced during the summer and autumn and the flowering stems die back during winter to be replaced by new growth in late winter. Some stems stay alive towards the base and produce new leaves from nodes along the stems during the winter. Seed production is usually prolific. Seed production per plant is in the tens of thousands in its first year of growth (when reproduces) and hundreds of thousands in its second year of growth (3).<br> Sources of information:<br>1\.
