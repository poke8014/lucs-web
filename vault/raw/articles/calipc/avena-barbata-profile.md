## Avena barbata

![Avena barbata_slender oat_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Avena-barbata_slender-oat_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** slender oat

_Avena barbata_(slender wild oat) is a winter annual grass (family Poaceae) that grows in nearly every grassland area of the state. It does well in sandy/poor soils, often on the road verges. It is one of the annual grasses that was introduced as a forage species and has replaced the native perennial grasses. It is native to Asia and Mediterranean Europe where it is located in grass meadows and within agricultural landscapes. It is an early pioneer grass species to freshly disturbed ground, especially when not shaded.
