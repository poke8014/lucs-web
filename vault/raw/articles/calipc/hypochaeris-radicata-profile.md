## Hypochaeris radicata

![Hypochaeris radicata_common catsear_flower_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Hypochaeris-radicata_common-catsear_flower_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** rough cat's-ear; false dandelion

_Hypochaeris radicata_ (rough cat’s-ear, hairy dandelion) is a perennial herb/forb (family Asteraceae) that is native to Europe. It is a weed of disturbed sites across a wide range of habitats in California and can invade intact coastal terrace prairie and scrub communities, where observations suggest it displaces natives. Plants are characterized by having perennial habit, more highly toothed/lobed leaves, larger flower heads, and only one fruit shape (an achene with a long, thin beak), compared to its non-native congener, _H. glabra_.  Achenes are wind blown and can spread easily.
