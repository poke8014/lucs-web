## Chondrilla juncea

![Chondrilla juncea_rush skeletonweed_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Chondrilla-juncea_rush-skeletonweed_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** skeleton weed; devil's grass; hogbite; gum succory; naked weed

_Chondrilla juncea_ (rush skeletonweed) is a perennial or biennial forb (family Asteraceae) and can be found scattered throughout California but is considered an uncommon weed. It can grow in disturbed soils of roadsides, croplands, especially irrigated grain fields, semi-arid pastures, rangelands, and residential properties. Plants are highly competitive for water and nutrients. It is native to Europe, Asia, and Africa and was accidentally introduced to the U. S. as a contaminant of fodder in 1914. Rush skeletonweed is thought to have originated in the submontane region surrounding the Caspian Sea, where the genus _Chondrilla_ is most diverse.
