## Emex spinosa

![Emex spinosa by Ron Vanderhoff](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Emex-spinosa-by-Ron-Vanderhoff-1.jpg)Photo: Ron Vanderhoff

**Synonyms:** Rumex spinosus

**Common names:** devil's thorn; spiny threecornerjack

_Emex spinosa_ (spiny _Emex_, devil’s thorn) is an annual (family Polygonaceae) found on California’s south coast. This Mediterranean native is not yet common in California, but it is spreading rapidly and is known to crowd out native species. It frequently infests disturbed areas, especially in coastal habitats. _Emex_ _spinosa_’s spiny seed pods stick to people and animals, so it spreads quickly along trails and then into undisturbed areas.
