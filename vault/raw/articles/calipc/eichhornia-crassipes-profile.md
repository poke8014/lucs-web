## Eichhornia crassipes

![Eichhornia_crassipes_ by By Wouter Hagens](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Eichhornia_crassipes_-by-By-Wouter-Hagens.jpg)Photo: Wouter Hagens

**Synonyms:** Eichhornia speciosa, Heteranthera formosa, Piaropus crassipes, Pontederia crassipes

**Common names:** water hyacinth

_Eichhornia crassipes_ (water hyacinth) is a floating freshwater perennial (family Pontederiaceae) that jams rivers and lakes with tons of floating plant matter. Floating mats of this plant can weigh up to 200 tons per acre. In California, _Eichhornia crassipes_ typically is found below 660 feet (200 m) elevation in the Central Valley, San Francisco Bay Area, and south coast.
