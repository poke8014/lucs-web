## Cynosurus echinatus

![Cynosurus echinatus_hedgehog dogtail grass_ JM Di Tomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Cynosurus-echinatus_hedgehog-dogtail-grass_-JM-Di-Tomaso.jpg)Photo courtesy Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** hedgehog dogtail; annual dogtail; bristly dogtail grass; hedgehoggy

_Cynosurus echinatus_ (hedgehog dogtail grass) is an annual or short-lived perennial grass (family Poaceae) that is native to southern Europe and has been introduced and established to the Americas and Australia. It flowers in summer and can be found at lower elevations along trails and disturbed areas in both open and wooded areas. This species can be distinguished by having awns and flowers that all point in one direction on the inflorescence, giving it a bristly look. _Cynosurus echinatus_ can produce monocultures, reduce forage desirability, and increase fire hazard by leaving persistent thatch.
