## Carrichtera annua

![Carrichtera annua_Flora y Vegetacion Wikimedia](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Carrichtera-annua_Flora-y-Vegetacion-Wikimedia.jpeg)Photo: Wikimedia

**Synonyms:** Vella annua

**Common names:** Ward's weed

_Carrichtera annua_ (Ward’s weed) is an annual herb (family Brassicaceae ) with white to yellow flowers and hairy lobed leaves found in the south coast ranges of California. It is native to the Mediterranean and southwestern Asia. It favors grasslands and scrub and chaparral habitat. It reproduces by seed only. Seeds are dispersed by foraging animals, vehicles, agricultural activities and humans.
