## Nicotiana glauca

![Nicotiana glauca_tree tobacco_ JM Di Tomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Nicotiana-glauca_tree-tobacco_-JM-Di-Tomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** tree tobacco

_Nicotiana glauca_ (tree tobacco) is a tree/shrub (family Solanaceae), which stands 10-20 feet tall and is short-lived. Tree tobacco was introduced to California about 100 years ago and is found growing up to 5,000 feet in disturbed soils, vacant lots, along roadsides, streamsides, and other riparian areas.
