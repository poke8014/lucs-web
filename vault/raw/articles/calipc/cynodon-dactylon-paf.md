| Describe key reproductive characteristics:<br>In California, flowering occurs from March to August. C. dactylon is wind-pollinatied, and can produce 230 seeds per panicle during the first three months after the initial seed set, but is considered a very sparse seed producer except for some biotypes as in California, where the cultivated biotype has a seedset of 95%. Seed in Australia remains viable in the soil for 3-4 years. The axillary buds at the nodes of rhizomes and stolons provide the principal means of spreading and dispersal. Rhizomes can be superficial or very deep from a few centimeters to over a meter in depth, which allows it to survive a wide range of climatic conditions from flooding to droughts.
