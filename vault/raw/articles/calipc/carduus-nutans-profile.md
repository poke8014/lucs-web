## Carduus nutans

![Carduus nutans_Musk Thistle_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Carduus-nutans_Musk-Thistle_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** musk thistle; giant plumeless thistle; nodding (plumeless) thistle;

_Carduus nutans_ (musk thistle) is a biennial or winter annual (family Asteraceae) found in disturbed open sites, roadsides, pastures, annual grasslands, and waste areas, but is mostly limited to the Klamath and Cascade Ranges, northern Sierra Nevada, and Modoc Plateau in California. Previous populations in southern California were eradicated. _Carduus nutans_ is native to Western Europe and was accidentally introduced into the United States in the early 1900s.
