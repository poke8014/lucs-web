In Contra Costa County, Trillium ovatum would disappear from the area one to two years after vinca invaded (5). Comparative studies on vinca found that all native and non-native plants except walnut and box elder occurred in lower densities within plots invaded with vinca compared to uninvaded plots (6). Vinca grows quickly in moist soil and spreads rapidly.<br> Sources of information: |
