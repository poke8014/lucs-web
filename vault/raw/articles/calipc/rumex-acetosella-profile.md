## Rumex acetosella

![Rumex acetosella_red sorrell_ JM Di Tomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Rumex-acetosella_red-sorrell_-JM-Di-Tomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Synonyms:** Acetosella vulgaris

**Common names:** sheep sorrel

_Rumex acetosella_ (red sorrel, sheep sorrel) is a perennial forb/herb (family Polygonaceae). In California, sheep sorrel occurs in a freshwater marsh community and is common in annual grasslands, montane meadows and perennial bunchgrass communities.
