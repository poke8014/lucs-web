## Avena fatua

![Avena fatua_wildoat_ JM Di Tomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Avena-fatua_wildoat_-JM-Di-Tomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** wild oats

_Avena fatua_ (wild oat) is a winter annual grass (family Poaceae) that is a common agricultural weed. It grows in most grassland sites within the state, particularly in sandy/poor soils, often on the road verges. It is one of the annual grasses that was introduced as a forage species and has replaced the native perennial grasses. This oat is native to Eurasia.
