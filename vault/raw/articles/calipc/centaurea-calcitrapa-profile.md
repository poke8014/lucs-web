## Centaurea calcitrapa

![Centaurea calcitrapa_purple starthistle_flowering head_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Centaurea-calcitrapa_purple-starthistle_flowering-head_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** purple starthistle

_Centaurea calcitrapa_ (purple starthistle) grows as an annual, biennial or perennial (family Asteraceae) in fields, roadsides, disturbed open sites, grasslands, overgrazed rangelands, and logged areas in the northern and central coast ranges of California. It is native to Europe, and, like other _Centaurea_ species, may produce allelopathic effects. It is highly competitive with other plants, often displacing desired vegetation.
