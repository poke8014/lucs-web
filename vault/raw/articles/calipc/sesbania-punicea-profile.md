## Sesbania punicea

![](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Sesbania_punicea_FWallace.jpg)Photo: Frank Wallace

**Synonyms:** Sesbania punicea, Sesbania tripetii, Daubentonia punicea

**Common names:** scarlet _Wisteria_; red _Sesbania_; rattlebox; Chinese _Wisteria_

_Sesbania punicea_ (red _Sesbania_, scarlet _Wisteria_) is a deciduous shrub or small tree (family Fabaceae), up to 4 meters tall. _Sesbania punicea_ is mostly found in riparian areas in the Central Valley, forming clusters so thick that access to the river becomes difficult to impossible. It displaces native plants used by wildlife and contributes to bank erosion and flooding.
