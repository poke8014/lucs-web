## Carthamus lanatus

![Carthamus lanatus_Woolly distaff thistle_flowerhead_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Carthamus-lanatus_Woolly-distaff-thistle_flowerhead_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Synonyms:** Carthamus lanatus ssp. lanatus

**Common names:** woolly distaff thistle; false starthistle; saffron thistle; woolly safflower; woolly starthistle

_Carthamus lanatus_ (woolly distaff thistle) is a winter annual forb (family Asteraceae) found in disturbed open sites, roadsides, pastures, annual grasslands, and waste areas. Its spiny foliage can injure livestock. It is primarily found in the northern coast ranges of California. It is native to the Mediterranean and Europe.
