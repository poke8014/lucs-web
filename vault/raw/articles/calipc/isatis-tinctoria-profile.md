## Isatis tinctoria

![Isatis tinctoria_Dyers woad_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Isatis-tinctoria_Dyers-woad_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** dyer's woad

_Isatis tinctoria_ (dyer’s woad) is a winter biennial or short-lived annual herb/forb (family Brassicaceae). Plants are highly competitive and often grow in dense colonies. It can be found distributed among the Klamath, Cascade, and North Coast Ranges, northern & central Sierra Nevada, Modoc Plateau, and northern San Francisco Bay region. Dyer’s woad is a noxious weed of rangeland, agronomic crops, and undisturbed natural areas.
