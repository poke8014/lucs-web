## Cotoneaster lacteus

![Cotoneaster_lacteus_milkflower cotoneaster_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Cotoneaster_lacteus_milkflower-cotoneaster_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Synonyms:** Cotoneaster parneyi

**Common names:** milkflower _Cotoneaster_; Parney's _Cotoneaster_

_Cotoneaster lacteus_ (Parney’s _Cotoneaster_) is an evergreen shrub (family Rosaceae) found along the central and north coast of California. Parney’s _Cotoneaster_, which has white flowers and red fruits, was introduced from China as an ornamental plant, and has escaped cultivation. It is found in disturbed places, especially near residential sites, as well as undisturbed coastal scrub, grassland and forests. Each plant can produce thousands of fruits every year, which are distributed by birds, small mammals, water movement and human activities. Mechanical removal of the shrubs can provide good control, but follow-up is needed, as the plants may resprout from the roots.
