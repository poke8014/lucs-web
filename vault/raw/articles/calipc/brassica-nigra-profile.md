## Brassica nigra

![](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Brassica-nigra-Bommer-Trail-3-3-12-8.jpg)Photo: Ron Vanderhoff

**Synonyms:** Sinapis nigra, Mutarda nigra

**Common names:** black mustard

_Brassica nigra_ (black mustard) is a winter annual herb/forb (family Brassicaceae). Like other mustards, black mustard grows profusely and produces allelopathic chemicals that prevent germination of native plants. The spread of black mustard can increase the frequency of fires in chaparral and coastal sage scrub, changing these habitats to annual grassland. It is native to regions of North Africa, temperate regions of Europe, and parts of Asia.
