## Carpobrotus edulis

![Carpobrotus edulis_hottentot fig_JM DiTomaso](https://www.cal-ipc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/Carpobrotus-edulis_hottentot-fig_JM-DiTomaso.jpg)Photo: Joseph DiTomaso

**Common names:** highway iceplant

_Carpobrotus edulis_ (Hottentot-fig or iceplant) is a succulent shrub (family Aizoaceae) found throughout coastal California and the Channel Islands, especially in areas with a warm winter climate. Native to South Africa and introduced as an ornamental plant, _Carpobrotus edulis_ now inhabits coastal scrub, grasslands, chaparral, bluffs, dunes and beaches where it creates dense mats that increase soil organic matter over time, allowing new non-native species to invade. _Carpobrotus edulis_ propagates by seed and vegetatively. Even small stem fragments can regenerate into a new plant, making control difficult.
